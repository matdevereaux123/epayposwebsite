Before you shoot

  • File names matter. Save each one exactly as the left column reads — the pages already point at those paths, so a correct name means no code change.
  • Merch and equipment shoot square on a plain white background. The shop cards crop to a square, so anything landscape loses its sides.
  • Reach is the priority order. The hat, hoodie, collared shirt and tote each appear on 9 pages; the cash drawer and the handheld on 7. Golf tees, ball markers, pens and holsters appear on 2 — shoot those last.
  • Cash drawer and card tray had stand-in illustrations built. They were not good enough and have been pulled, so both slots are empty again and waiting on the real branded units.
  • The handheld model is unconfirmed. PAX's US site lists no A50 or A85 — their current handheld without a printer is the A77. Confirm the model and the photo can come straight from the manufacturer.
