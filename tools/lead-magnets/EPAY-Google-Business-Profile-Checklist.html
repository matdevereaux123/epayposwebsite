Google Business Profile & Reviews Checklist

Step 1

Claim and verify your profile

  • Search your business name on Google Maps. If a listing exists, choose "Claim this business". If not, add it at business.google.com.
  • Sign in with a Google account the business controls, not a former employee's or an agency's personal one.
  • Complete verification. Google chooses the method (for example a code, a call or a short video). Your profile isn't fully live until it's verified.
  • Add a second owner or manager, so you're never locked out if one person leaves.

Step 3

Photos that sell

  • Logo and a cover photo
  • The outside, as customers see it from the street
  • The inside: dining room, bar, counter or aisles
  • Your best-selling dishes or products
  • Your team at work (with their OK)
  • New photos every month, taken in good light

Step 4

Getting more reviews, the right way

Do

  • Get your review link: in your Business Profile, find "Ask for reviews" and copy the link Google gives you.
  • Ask at the right moment: right after a great meal, a compliment or a smooth checkout.
  • Make it easy: put the link in a QR code on table tents, the counter or the door, and in follow-up texts or emails.
  • Ask everyone. Don't only ask the customers you think are happy.
  • Train staff on one simple line, e.g. "If you enjoyed it, a Google review really helps us."

Don't (these break Google's review policies)

  • Don't offer discounts, freebies or anything else in exchange for reviews.
  • Don't write reviews for your own business or ask staff or family to.
  • Don't screen customers so that only happy ones are asked to review.
  • Don't buy reviews or use a service that posts them for you.

Step 5

Replying to reviews

Reply to every review, good and bad, within a few days. Future customers read your replies as closely as the reviews. Keep it short, specific and human.

Five starsThanks so much, [name]! Glad you loved the [dish / product they mentioned]. We'll see you next time. [Your name], [role]
Good, with a small complaintThanks for coming in, [name], and for the honest feedback about [issue]. We've shared it with the team and we're on it. Hope to see you again. [Your name]
Negative[Name], I'm sorry your visit wasn't what it should have been. That's not the standard we hold ourselves to. I'd like to make it right: please reach me directly at [phone or email]. [Your name], [role]
Not a real customer / wrong businessWe couldn't find a record of this visit and want to look into it. Please contact us at [phone or email] so we can help. [Your name]

Ground rules

  • Never argue in public, share a customer's details, or blame staff by name.
  • Take problems offline: give a direct way to reach a manager.
  • If a review breaks Google's policies (spam, offensive, or clearly about another business), report it from your profile instead of arguing with it.

This checklist is general guidance. Google's features and policies change, so check Google's current Business Profile help pages. © EPAY POS.
