Your break-even point

Fixed costs stay the same whatever you sell (rent, insurance, salaried staff). Variable costs rise with sales (food, hourly labor, processing). Write the variable costs as a share of sales, then:

Break-even sales per month = fixed costs ÷ (1 − variable costs as a % of sales)

Section 9

Launch checklist

60–90 days out

  • Lease signed, build-out scheduled
  • Licenses and permits applied for
  • Equipment and POS ordered
  • Suppliers set up
  • Payment processing account approved

30 days out

  • Staff hired, training dates set
  • Menu finalized and priced
  • Menu built into the POS
  • Social pages and Google listing live

Opening week

  • Health inspection passed
  • POS and card terminals tested with real sales
  • Friends-and-family soft opening
  • End-of-day closeout practiced
  • Opening announcement sent
