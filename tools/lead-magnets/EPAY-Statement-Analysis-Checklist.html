Statement Analysis Checklist

Step 1

Find your effective rate

Processors name their fees differently, so comparing fee names gets you nowhere. One number cuts through all of it: your effective rate, meaning total fees as a share of total card sales. Take both numbers from the summary section of the same monthly statement.

Total card sales this month
Total fees this month (every line)
Effective rate = fees ÷ sales × 100

Do this for three months in a row. One month can be thrown off by an annual fee or an unusual week, but three months shows the real pattern.

Step 2

Know the three parts of every card fee

1. Interchange

Set by the card networks and paid to the bank that issued the card. It changes with the card type (debit, rewards, business) and how it was taken (tapped, dipped, keyed).

2. Network fees

Charged by Visa, Mastercard, Discover and American Express on your volume and transactions. Often labeled "assessments."

3. Processor markup

What your processor adds on top. This is the part that differs between processors, and the part you can change.

Step 4

Red flags to circle

  • Your effective rate went up, but your card mix and sales didn't change.
  • A fee appeared that wasn't there last month, or one got bigger without a notice you remember.
  • Lines you can't match to anything in your agreement.
  • A large share of sales billed at "mid-qualified" or "non-qualified" rates.
  • A PCI non-compliance fee month after month.
  • An equipment lease that costs more over its term than the equipment is worth, or that you can't cancel.
  • Charges for services you don't use, such as a gateway when you only take cards in person.

Step 5

Questions to ask your processor

  1. What pricing model am I on: interchange-plus, tiered or flat?
  2. What exactly is your markup, as a percentage and per transaction?
  3. Can you walk me through every fee on this statement, one line at a time?
  4. Which fees can change, and how much notice do you give?
  5. Do I have an early termination fee or an equipment lease, and when does each end?
  6. When does my money land in my account after I close the day?
